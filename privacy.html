Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information YASR Tattoo collects through yasrtattoo.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it simple: we only collect what we need to answer your appointment request.

1. Who we are

YASR Tattoo is the tattoo practice of artist Yaşar Tokyay, based in Fayetteville, North Carolina, USA. For any privacy question, contact us at yasr.tatweb@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you send an appointment request, we receive your full name, phone number, email address, your tattoo idea, and the language you used on the site. If you email us reference photos, we receive those photos and your email address.

Information collected automatically. Like almost every website, our hosting and security providers automatically process basic technical data when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you open. This is used to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We also count visits with an anonymous statistics tool that does not identify you personally.

We do not ask for sensitive information, payment details or government ID numbers through this website. Please do not include them in your message.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your request and arrange, plan and confirm your appointment.
  • To contact you about your appointment by phone, text or email.
  • To keep the website working, secure and free of spam.
  • To understand, in anonymous form, how many people visit the site.

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not send marketing messages.

4. Service providers we use

We use a few trusted companies to run this website. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Formspree receives the appointment form and forwards it to our email.
  • Google (Gmail) is our email provider. Fonts on this site are also loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Vercel hosts the website and provides anonymous visitor statistics.
  • Cloudflare delivers the site and protects it against attacks.
  • WhatsApp may be used by our team to share your appointment details internally so we can organize your appointment.

Our Instagram link takes you to Instagram, which has its own privacy policy.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our visitor statistics work without cookies. The only thing stored in your browser is your language choice (English, Spanish or German), so the site opens in the same language next time. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser data.

Do Not Track: because we do not track you across other websites, we treat all visitors the same way whether or not a "Do Not Track" signal is sent.

6. Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with the service providers listed above, or when required by law.

7. How long we keep it

We keep appointment requests and related emails only as long as needed to arrange and provide your tattoo service and to keep basic records. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

8. Security

The site is served only over a secure HTTPS connection, and the form includes spam protection. No method of sending data over the internet is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

9. Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the personal information you sent us, or to stop contacting you. Just email yasr.tatweb@gmail.com. We will reply as soon as possible and will not treat you differently for making a request.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws (for example in California or the European Union). If you contact us from outside the United States, please note that your information is processed in the United States. We use it only because you asked us to contact you about an appointment.

10. Age requirement

Our tattoo services are only for adults aged 18 and over. This website is not intended for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with its effective date.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or your information? Email us at yasr.tatweb@gmail.com.
