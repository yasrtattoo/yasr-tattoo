The Artist

YASR is a tattoo artist with over 13 years of experience, known for bold custom work, large-scale compositions, realism, color, and complex cover-ups.

For him, tattooing is not about copying an image onto skin. Every piece is designed around the person, their body, their story, and their energy. His approach combines strong composition, detail, contrast, and originality to create tattoos that are immediately recognizable.

After building his career in Turkey and continuing his journey across the United States, YASR has developed a style shaped by years of experience, constant evolution, and one simple rule: never stop creating.
